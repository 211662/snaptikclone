Our Story

Founded in 2025, NHP STORE LLC is a technology company specializing in multimedia tools and video download solutions. Our flagship product, SnapTik, has quickly become one of the leading TikTok video downloaders, providing fast and reliable service to millions of users globally.

We started with a simple mission: to provide users with fast, reliable, and free tools to download and save their favorite social media content. As a growing company, we are committed to innovation and excellence in the video download technology space.

Our Mission

To democratize access to social media content by providing free, high-quality video download tools while respecting creators rights and platform guidelines.

Our Vision

To become the worlds most trusted platform for social media content management and download solutions, powered by cutting-edge technology and a passion for user experience.
