Our Story

Founded in 2019, NHP STORE LLC is a technology company specializing in multimedia tools and video download solutions. Our flagship product, SnapTik, has become one of the world's leading TikTok video downloaders, trusted by millions of users globally.

We started with a simple mission: to provide users with fast, reliable, and free tools to download and save their favorite social media content. What began as a small project has evolved into a comprehensive platform serving over 10 million users monthly across 180+ countries.

Our Mission

To democratize access to social media content by providing free, high-quality video download tools while respecting creators' rights and platform guidelines.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted platform for social media content management and download solutions.
